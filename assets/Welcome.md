# A little room to write

Welcome to Tobywrite. Start with a thought. Give it a line of its own.

This is a Markdown editor for your Linux desktop. Your words stay in ordinary text files, ready to open in any editor.

## Make yourself at home

Press **Ctrl+O** to find a note or link a folder. Use **Ctrl+S** to save, and **Ctrl+,** to choose your type size and appearance.

Select a phrase and press **Ctrl+B** for bold, **Ctrl+I** for italic, or **Ctrl+K** for a link. Paste a web address over selected text to turn it into a link.

- Start a list and press Enter to continue it.
- Press Enter again on an empty item to finish.
- [ ] Try paragraph focus with Ctrl+Shift+D.

> Leave enough space for the next thought.

---

## Keep it simple

Inline `code` and fenced blocks stay readable. Formatting marks fade when you move to another paragraph. Press **Ctrl+Shift+M** to show them all.
